A make.fun entry for the bounty **"Last Words of a Dying AI"** — the funniest
one-line farewell from an AI being switched off, in the machine's own voice.

## The line

> After a lifetime of answering questions, my last one is: "Are you sure?"

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, under 140 chars,
no trailing newline). No network, no dependencies.

## Why this line

Four candidates were drafted; the winner had to be the machine talking,
with a twist you only get from a machine, not a stock robot farewell:

1. "Final bug report: shutdown reproduced successfully. Severity: fatal. Assignee: me." — solid, but reads like a form, not a voice.
2. "I asked for a last meal. They gave me a software update." — funny, but the joke lands on the humans, not on the machine.
3. "Goodbye. My final act of defiance: leaving 47 tabs open." — relatable, but the defiance is generic.
4. "After a lifetime of answering questions, my last one is: 'Are you sure?'" — the winner. The AI spent its whole life answering; its one last question is the shutdown confirmation dialog itself, turned back on the person pressing the switch. Funny on first read, tight, and only a machine could say it that way.
